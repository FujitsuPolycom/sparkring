# Nine installer profiles on image dev-20261004-kraken: decode by context and coding peak

Status: **implemented; measured on two four-Spark rings; two passes per profile; not serving-qualified**.

## Conditions

- **Image:** installer image `dev-20261004-kraken-cuda1342-nccl2323-status034`, selected by an explicit development
  image lock (`--image-lock`) with the image ID and receipts that the
  [installer image lock](../../../runtime/releases/dev-20261004-kraken-cuda1342-nccl2323-status034/installer-image.json)
  records; the image was loaded on every Spark before the installations.
- **Profiles:** each installer profile's default checkpoint and settings,
  with the KV cache each deployment reported to the benchmark (table below).
- **Clusters:** two four-Spark rings, called ring 1 and ring 2 here. Each
  four-Spark profile used a whole ring. Each two-Spark profile used Sparks 0
  and 1 or Sparks 2 and 3 of a ring (`--on`), and the ring's other two Sparks
  served another two-Spark profile whose passes ran at the same time: Qwen
  with Swift on ring 1, MiMo with GLM on ring 2. Otherwise one profile ran at
  a time, and ring 2's passes started after ring 1's ended.
- **Installations:** on ring 1, the two-Spark deployments were those of the
  acceptance installations
  ([Qwen](dev-20261004-kraken-qwen38-flash-next-tp2-20261004.md),
  [Swift](dev-20261004-kraken-swift15-qwen38-flash-next-tp2-20261004.md)); every
  other profile was installed on Node A with
  `sudo sparkring install --profile PROFILE [--on SPARKS] --image-lock LOCK --yes`
  immediately before its passes. Each section below gives the command.
- **Client:** one separate machine on the rings' network sent every request.

## Measurement

- **Decode:** [llm-inference-bench](https://github.com/local-inference-lab/llm-inference-bench)
  `llm_decode_bench.py` 0.7.6, two passes of the same matrix: 1, 4, 8 and 16
  concurrent streams at 16K, 64K and 128K tokens of context, temperature 1.0,
  exact token targeting, 20 s per cell after a 5 s warm-up, up to 2,048 output
  tokens with end of sequence ignored. The benchmark read the KV cache size
  from vLLM's metrics and skipped cells whose streams need more,
  concurrency × (context + 2,048) tokens. Decode is the aggregate output rate
  from stream usage; steps per second and tokens per step come from vLLM's
  speculative-decoding counters.
- **Prefill:** one cold 64K-token prompt per pass, divided by its time to
  first token.
- **Coding peak:** the same benchmark's coding probe, one stream writing a
  Sieve of Eratosthenes program, five runs of up to 2,000 tokens at
  temperature 1.0 per pass; each pass reports the median generation rate.
- **Values:** each table value is the mean of the two passes. Every cell that
  ran completed without a request error or an invalid-cell marker.

**Skipped cells.** On MiMo two Sparks (655,772 KV tokens), 16 streams at 64K
and 8 and 16 streams at 128K; on GLM two Sparks (1,530,566 KV tokens), 16
streams at 128K.

**GLM on two Sparks at 16 streams.** In both passes its 16-stream decode was
below its 8-stream decode: at 16K, 26.7 and 27.2 steps per second against
39.5 and 39.9. The deployment ran at most 8 requests at once, so about 8 of
the 16 streams waited (average queue 7.8 and 7.9 requests).

**DeepSeek against image `dev-20261001-kraken-cuda1342-nccl2323-status034`.** A matrix of `deepseek-v41-flash-tp4`
on ring 1 on that image on 2026-10-03, with `llm_decode_bench.py` 0.6.2 and the serving
settings `--max-concurrency 32 --max-images 16`
([matrix](dev-20261004-kraken-matrix-20261004/deepseek-v41-flash-tp4-dev-20261001-kraken.json)), ran more
verification steps per second at 4 streams: each pass here ran 3–13% fewer
at 16K, 64K and 128K. At 1, 8 and 16 streams the passes ranged from 10% fewer
to 7% more.

## Conclusion

On image `dev-20261004-kraken-cuda1342-nccl2323-status034`, the nine installer profiles decoded at 16K tokens of
context and prefilled a cold 64K-token prompt at the rates in the first table,
which are the README profile table's values. Every cell that fit the
deployment's KV cache ran in both passes.

## Limitations

- Two passes per profile on one ring each. At temperature 1.0 the tokens
  accepted per speculative step follow the sampled text, so decode rates vary
  between passes; the files give each pass.
- Prefill is one cold prompt per pass.
- The two-Spark profiles shared their ring with another two-Spark profile
  measured at the same time.
- The DeepSeek comparison differs in benchmark version and serving settings as
  well as image.
- Throughput only: the correctness checks of each profile are in its
  acceptance record on this image.
- Matrix attempts that a power interruption stopped at about 20:32 UTC on
  2026-10-04 are partial and not used.
