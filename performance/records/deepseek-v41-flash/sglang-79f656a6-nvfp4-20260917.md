# SGLang cycle: adapter 79f656a6, serving controls, and the NVFP4 checkpoint (2026-09-17)

Status: **implemented**. One contributor site, one day, nine boots on the same four-Spark
direct cycle. The record attributes each measured change to one variable and reports the
bounded gates that ran on every boot. It does not qualify unattended production deployment, does
not change the recipe defaults, and does not establish benchmark parity for the NVFP4 variant.

## Conditions

Four GB10 DGX Sparks cabled as a direct four-node cycle, TP4/EP4, SparkRing patched NCCL 2.30.7
mounted in place over SGLang's pip NCCL, local NVMe checkpoints and Mia-layout packed Engram
files on every rank, seven-key bearer authentication, `--enable-metrics`. Two images built from
Mia's Dockerfile over the pinned ARM64 SGLang base (`e087e662` SGLang):

|---|---|---|
| "old" | `e59e6eb67479aa68f6fa700c600dc90a0729b5ec` | the 2026-09-11 record's image |

Common profile unless a row says otherwise: context 655360, chunk 4096, eight requests, DSpark
block 5 without SPS/STS tables, b12x MXFP8 dense backend, fused MoE finalize off, expandable
segments off, 96 Engram I/O threads, zero row cache, decoder replay on, plus the site's SGLang
memory overlays (upstream #39068 and the indexer-logits budget change) that the 2026-09-13
comment on #267 describes. Ranks were **not** rebooted between boots; containers were stopped
and restarted. Prompts for needles are `v41needle.py`'s deterministic filler with the needle at
50 % depth; a repeated identical prompt hits the radix cache, so each table says whether a
number is cold or warm.

## Measurement

Prefill ladders: 16K and 64K prompts, three runs each, one-token answers, median server-reported
prompt tokens / wall. Decode ladders: 256 fixed tokens at C1/C4/C8, three runs, median aggregate.
Needles: one request each, time to first token. Concurrency probe: N distinct ~131K or ~262K
prompts sent at once (`sglang-deep-concurrency-probe.py`, streaming, 32-token answers, exactness
checked). Exact-output gate: 20 fixed prompts including four ~12–15K prompts, first-token top-20
log-probabilities and 48-token greedy continuations, compared to the 2026-09-11 fixture
`sgl-replay0.json`. Structured gate: 112 tool/JSON requests (48 serial, 8 waves of 8;
chat/stream/raw). One observation per cell unless stated; no confidence intervals.

## 2. Admission delayer off and a larger pool (boot 2: fraction 0.80, `--max-total-tokens 7000000`, `--min-free-slots-delay 1`)

SGLang classes DSpark as DFlash-family and, at `--max-running-requests 8`, holds new prefills
until two slots are free (`srt/managers/min_free_slots_delayer.py`), which is the 17 s C8 wait
above. The boot budget line at 0.80 read `full_token=8235520`, so an 8,000,000 pin would have
fitted by 235K tokens; 7,000,000 was pinned and honoured (`max_total_num_tokens=6999808`).

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| auth, structured | pass, 112/112 |
| prefill 16K / 64K | 3,497 / 3,473 tok/s |
| decode C1 / C4 / C8 | 35.2 / 77.6 / **104.0** tok/s; C8 worst first-token wait **1.2 s** |
| needles 131K ×2 (cold, then a warm repeat that had been flushed — see below) / 262K / 655K | 53.1 / 50.6 s · 125.0 s · 352.9 s |
| C4 × 131K distinct | 4/4, batch 194.9 s; first tokens at 50.6 / 97.0 / 145.8 / 194.5 s |
| C8 × 131K | 8/8, batch 202.6 s (4 of 8 prompts hit the radix cache: the probe reused seeds across runs; fixed afterwards) |
| **C8 × 262K distinct — 2.08M live tokens** | **8/8**, batch 830.4 s; first tokens 119.9 → 829.5 s in ~103 s steps; head `MemAvailable` low-water 13 GiB, no guard trip |

Two runtime properties this exposed, neither new to this image: prefills are strictly serial
(`max_prefill_tokens` 16384, FCFS), and decode makes no progress while another request prefills —
every request's 32-token answer arrived only when the batch's last prefill finished. A larger pool
fixes the capacity case (8 × 262K cannot fit 1.5M), not the latency case. The C8 decode gain from
the delayer (+21 %) reproduced on every later boot with the flag.

## 3. Rejected: 1024-token prefill chunk with 1M context (boot 3: chunk 1024, context 1048576, pool 7,000,000, fraction 0.80, delayer off)

Mia's TP4 profile ships this chunk to fit the indexer's per-chunk transient at 1M context. On
this cycle it costs prefill throughput at every size, so the site rejected it. The 1M needle did
pass — the only 1M-context data point for this runtime.

prefill bytes at a few GB/s, so four times the chunk count is the plausible mechanism; a switched

## 6. Distribution comparison: NVFP4 versus stock on a 1,440-position panel

The 2026-09-11 exact-output gate compares 20 prompts at one position each. To see where the two
checkpoints differ, a 1,440-position panel was captured on both, on the same image and profile
(boots 5 and 8 above; a second NVFP4 boot, boot 9, repeated the capture as a floor).

**Panel.** 24 windows of 1,024 tokens: 16 prose/Markdown documents and 8 Python standard-library
modules, tokenized through the server's `/tokenize` (no chat template, no BOS) and sent as
token-id prompts to `/v1/completions` at 60 prefix lengths per window (64, 80, …, 1008) with
`max_tokens 1`, `temperature 0`, `logprobs 20`. Prompt logprobs are unavailable on this runtime
(decoder replay), so this is 1,440 single-position next-token distributions. Reference = stock
checkpoint. Definitions: top-1 agreement is argmax equality; truncated KL renormalises both
top-20 sets over their own support and floors a reference token missing from the candidate's
top-20 at the candidate's 20th entry (only 4 % of positions had the whole reference top-20 inside
the candidate's; the stock top-20 holds 93 % of the mass on average), so it is indicative, not a
full-vocabulary divergence. Tools: [`topk-panel-build_panel.py.txt`](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/topk-panel-build_panel.py.txt),
[`drive_panel`](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/topk-panel-drive_panel.py.txt), [`score_panel`](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/topk-panel-score_panel.py.txt);
[panel token ids](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/panel.json); captures for [stock](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/panel-capture-stock.json)
and [NVFP4](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/panel-capture-nvfp4-boot3.json); scores [NVFP4 vs stock](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/panel-score-nvfp4-vs-stock.json)
and [NVFP4 boot vs boot](sglang-79f656a6-nvfp4-20260917/panel-score-nvfp4-boot3-vs-boot4.json).

| Metric (1,440 positions) | NVFP4 vs stock | NVFP4 boot 9 vs boot 8 (floor) |
|---|---|---|
| top-1 agreement | **0.9292** (prose windows 0.898, min 0.833; code windows 0.992, min 0.967) | **1.0000** — the two captures are byte-identical |
| stock's top-1 inside the candidate's top-5 | 0.9965 | 0.9979 (self) |
| truncated KL mean / median / p99 | 0.0293 / 0.0064 / 0.256 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| positions where stock's top-1 probability > 0.9 (720) | NVFP4 agrees at **0.9986** (one flip) | — |
| positions where NVFP4's top-1 probability > 0.9 (703) | stock agrees at **1.0000** | — |
| at the 102 disagreements: stock's top-1 probability, median | **0.197** (10 above 0.5, 1 above 0.9) | — |
| at the 102 disagreements: stock's top-1 − top-2 margin, median | **0.030** (0.844 over all positions) | — |

**Reading.** The NVFP4 experts change the argmax at 7 % of positions, but those positions are
the ones where the stock model itself is close to a coin flip: the median stock margin between
its first and second choice at a disagreement is 0.03 against 0.84 overall, and where either
model is confident (top-1 above 0.9) the other agrees at 99.9–100 %. Code windows are nearly
unaffected; prose, where next-token choice is inherently more spread, carries almost all of the
flips. This is consistent with a variant whose weights are a lossless re-encoding and whose only
lossy step is 4-bit activation quantisation with calibrated scales, and it is what a "benchmark
parity" claim would look like at the token level. It is not a benchmark result: the panel does
not test tasks, long outputs, reasoning traces or tool use, and the truncated KL is a floor. The
served NVFP4 stack is also fully deterministic across boots on this cycle (identical top-20
sets at every position), which the stock stack's 2026-09-12 repeatability record could not show.

## Limitations

- One site, one cycle, one day; no reboots between boots; one observation per cell except where
  three-run medians are stated. Same-day boot-to-boot spread on deep needles was about 10 %.
- The exact-output gate and the panel compare two served quantised stacks with a truncated
  top-20 view; neither is a full-vocabulary teacher comparison, and neither measures task quality.
  NVIDIA's parity claim for the NVFP4 checkpoint is for GB300 and was not tested here.
- Recipe defaults (context 262144, fraction 0.90, pool 1,500,000, `flashinfer_mxfp4`) are
  unchanged by this record; the settings above are optional and site-selected.
- The DSpark draft overlay is a one-branch change to an Apache-2.0 SGLang module generated from
  the selected image at `--prepare` time; it has been exercised on SM121 only.
