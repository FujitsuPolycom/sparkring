# B12X MXFP8 kernels for the Qwen3.8-Flash-Next MTP draft on two Sparks, 2026-09-30

Status: **research-only**. One pair, one profile, one image built for this
measurement; the image is not published and no profile selects it.

## Question

The step-5500 checkpoint of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4 (branch
`qad-step5500-ple1000`, revision `60215d26cf5e`) stores the MoE experts of its
MTP draft layer as MXFP8: E4M3 weights with one UE8M0 scale per 32 values. The
`qwen38-flash-next-tp2` profile runs those experts on vLLM's `humming` MoE
backend, which keeps the draft's activations in BF16 (W8A16). Two open pull
requests add a B12X backend for them that also quantizes the activations to
MXFP8 (W8A8): local-inference-lab/vllm#955 (vLLM backend `B12X_MXFP8`, selected
by `moe_backend` `b12x`) and local-inference-lab/b12x#449 (the kernels).

1. Does the profile serve correctly with the draft on B12X?
2. Is the engine faster: more verification steps per second?
3. The pull request reports, on two GB10 groups of TP=2 with
   llm-inference-bench 0.7.5 and one sample per cell, an MTP accept length of
   2.24 / 2.14 / 2.09 at 1 / 4 / 8 streams, where this pair measures about
   2.3 with `humming`. Do the draft's FP8 activations lower the accept length,
   or does the difference come from the rest of that setup?

## Conclusion

The hand-merged patch serves the profile with the draft's MXFP8 experts on
B12X, with the same functional and screen results as `humming`.

Engine speed is unchanged. Steps per second is equal at one stream (23.8 for
both) and within run-to-run spread at eight (85.7 against 86.0, ranges
overlapping): the choice of draft expert backend makes no measurable
difference to the verification step at one or eight streams. The pull
request's 23.4 / 86.8 steps per second at 1 / 8 streams agree with both
variants. Prefill of 64K and 128K prompts was about 1% slower with `b12x`
(3,612 against 3,639 and 3,311 against 3,344 tok/s, ranges not overlapping);
the draft layer also processes prompt tokens. This record did not measure
the parent image; [shm-spin-window-20260930](shm-spin-window-20260930.md)
reports 24.0 – 24.2 and 86.0 – 88.5 steps per second for `humming` on it, from
a different deployment on the same pair.

FP8 activations in the draft do not lower acceptance here: accept length at
eight streams was 2.35 with `b12x` and 2.37 with `humming`, and the seeded
probe's acceptance rate 0.556 – 0.572 against 0.561 – 0.574, with matching
per-position rates. The pull request's lower 2.09 at eight streams therefore
comes from a difference in its setup rather than from W8A8. Its configuration
is not stated beyond two TP=2 groups, step 5500, llm-inference-bench 0.7.5 and
one sample per cell. One candidate, not tested here: with greedy drafting at
temperature 1.0 this checkpoint accepted 1.97 tokens per step on prose against
2.16 with probabilistic drafting
([installer tuning record](installer-tuning-20260925.md#probabilistic-drafting)),
a drop of similar size.

Limits: one pair, three benchmark runs and two probe passes per variant, one
day; temperature 1.0 only; the B12X kernels were not tested in isolation on
SM121.
